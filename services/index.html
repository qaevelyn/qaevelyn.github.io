Verification, not vibes

Services

I run an independent verification practice built on the same doctrine this site is built on: nothing is claimed unless it can be shown, and every engagement ends in a receipt the client keeps. The work is AI-collaboration QA — auditing systems that were built with AI, testing the outputs people are asked to trust, and checking the documents those outputs produce before they reach a patient, a client, or the public record.

The published record doubles as the portfolio. Each line of service below links to a paper on this site that shows the method in public, on real systems, with the receipts left in.

What an engagement looks like

  • Audits of AI-assisted work. What a system actually does, against what it claims to do — the gap is the finding. See Who Tests the Testers and The Tester Is the Target.
  • Adversarial testing. Structured attempts to make the model lie, leak, or overreach, documented step by step so the results reproduce. See Three Days of Silence.
  • Documentation QA. Patient-facing and client-facing documents verified line by line — the prep sheet is the canonical case. See The Class of the Prep Sheet.
  • Platform evaluation. Criteria-first assessment of AI platforms for a specific workload, not vendor marketing. See the platform comparison paper.
  • Method, on the record. How the verification is done is itself published: methodology and white-paper methodology.
