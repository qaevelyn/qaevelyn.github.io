What an engagement looks like

  • Audits of AI-assisted work. What a system actually does, against what it claims to do — the gap is the finding. See Who Tests the Testers?™ and The Tester Is the Target™.
  • Adversarial testing. Structured attempts to make the model lie, leak, or overreach, documented step by step so the results reproduce. See Three Days of Silence™.
  • Documentation QA. Patient-facing and client-facing documents verified line by line — the prep sheet is the canonical case. See The Class of the Prep Sheet™.
  • Cyber Readiness Baseline. I use the Cyber Readiness Institute's Core Four framework to assess your organization's baseline (Passwords/MFA, Software Updates, Phishing, Secure Storage). The engagement ends with a completed CRI Playbook and a readiness receipt. See the CRI standard.

    • Cyber Readiness Institute — Program Completed. Certificate of Completion issued November 16, 2026. Cyber Readiness Institute.

  • Platform evaluation. Criteria-first assessment of AI platforms for a specific workload, not vendor marketing. See AI Platform Comparison for Sovereign Archive Development™.
