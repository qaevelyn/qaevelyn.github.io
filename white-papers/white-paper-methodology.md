## Executive Summary

This document describes the methodology I use to select, research, and publish white papers. It is the process by which every other white paper in this section exists.

My lens on the world is active, not passive. I am not a passive observer of technology, of AI, of the industry I build in. Everything I encounter — tools, papers, conversations, failures — passes through a filter. What survives the filter becomes a white paper.

This methodology defines the filter.

---

## I. The Filter

Every day I encounter:

- New AI tools, platforms, and systems
- Papers, articles, and commentary from the field
- Conversations with other practitioners
- Failures and successes in my own work

Most of it does not become a white paper. Most of it is noise.

**The filter is this:** Does this change what I know? Does it change how I build? Does it change what I believe?

If yes — it becomes a candidate.

The filter is not passive. It requires attention, evaluation, and judgment. I do not write about everything I encounter. I write about what matters.

---

## II. The Test

Before writing, I apply a four-part test to any candidate subject:

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is the subject verified through my own experience, not just read about? | Lived experience is the contribution |
| 2 | Is there a finding or a position that others can learn from? | A white paper argues; it doesn't just describe |
| 3 | Can I cite evidence — platforms tested, data collected, outcomes observed? | Unsupported claims are opinion, not research |
| 4 | Does it serve the practice of sovereign AI? | Alignment with the larger mission |

If the answer is yes to all four — it becomes a white paper.

---

## III. The Process

Every white paper follows the same path:

1. **Encounter** — something crosses my path
2. **Test** — I use it, evaluate it, work with it
3. **Document** — I record what I observed in real time
4. **Argue** — I take a position and defend it with evidence
5. **Publish** — I add it to the portfolio

The process is not theoretical. It is grounded in actual work. Every paper in this section is a record of something I did, not something I read about.

---

## IV. The Standard

Every paper is held to the same standard:

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | Real sources, cited correctly |
| **Voice** | The author's voice — not templated, not AI-generated |
| **Disclosure** | AI collaboration is disclosed on every paper |
| **Sovereignty** | The paper aligns with the principles of sovereign AI |
| **Citation** | References are verifiable and current |

---

## V. Why This Matters

White papers written by observers describe what is happening.

White papers written by practitioners describe what *should* happen.

I am not a passive observer. I build. I test. I document. What I write, I have lived.

The distinction matters because:

- Observers write summaries. Practitioners write findings.
- Observers describe trends. Practitioners shape them.
- Observers are quoted. Practitioners are cited.

This methodology is the difference between the two.
