Gemini NotebookLM — The Closed RAG That Hallucinated

Gemini NotebookLM is a closed RAG system. It is supposed to retrieve information from uploaded sources and generate accurate outputs. It did not.

Failure Example
Name Errors It mispronounced and misspelled names
Terminology Errors It conflated "RAG" and "FBA"
Hallucinations It generated content not supported by the sources
No Disclaimer It did not warn users about its errors

The Corrected Transcript

I generated a corrected transcript from the raw output. The changes included:

Error Correction
"Evelyn Carroll" "Evelyn Caro"
"RAG" (in place of "FBA") "FBA"
"Alama" "Ollama"
"Aval's" "Evelyn's"
"Perskalis" "AWS re/Start"

A closed RAG system should not make these errors. It should retrieve and cite. It did not.

Reason Why It Is a Problem

Lessons Learned

Conclusion

Google AI Studio failed. Gemini NotebookLM hallucinated. I had to correct its errors. Despite these failures, I earned the Google AI Professional Certificate. I hold the credential. I am a certified professional. And I still do not trust Google's AI tools.

I am not anti-Google. I am anti-tools that do not work as promised — even when they come with a certificate.
