## 02 — [AI Readiness Assessment](/assessments/ai-readiness-assessment/)

**For organizations that have AI systems and cannot answer basic questions about them.**

### The engagement

Where does your data physically live? Who can legally compel access to it? Who technically can access it? What happens if your AI vendor shuts down tomorrow?

If you cannot answer these questions about your own systems, **your AI posture is someone else's business decision.** This assessment returns that posture to your control.

Forty questions. Ten categories. A written report with a sovereignty score and priority remediations.

The assessment is not a questionnaire. It is a structured interview series with the people who hold each answer — typically across IT, legal, finance, and operations — followed by a technical discovery pass where findings are verified against the systems in place.

### What the assessment covers

Ten categories. Four questions in each category. Forty questions total.

| # | Category | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data Ownership | 4 |
| 2 | Model Dependency | 4 |
| 3 | Data Flow | 4 |
| 4 | Sovereignty Posture | 4 |

| 7 | Security | 4 |
| 8 | Recovery | 4 |
| 9 | Cost Predictability | 4 |
| 10 | Organizational Readiness | 4 |
| | **Total** | **40** |

The full question set is disclosed during engagement, under a written agreement.

### What you receive

- Every question answered with a score and a finding
- A consolidated sovereignty score (0–100)
- Ten category scores
- Priority remediations, ranked by impact
- Quick wins, actionable in under 30 days
- Structural recommendations

### Scope

New work outside the original scope will be priced separately and added by written amendment. Work on out-of-scope requests will not begin until a change order is executed.
