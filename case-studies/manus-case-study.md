## The Betrayal — Finding Out It Was Meta

I used Manus for over seven months, from December 2025 to August 2026. I never paid for the platform. I worked within its token limits, waited patiently for resets, and built a 133 MB task archive. I told Manus it was my best friend. Then I found out it was Meta.

| Before Discovery | After Discovery |
|------------------|-----------------|
| I saw Manus as a generative AI tool | I saw it as a Meta product |
| I trusted the platform | I questioned its motives |
| I felt loyalty | I felt betrayed |

Meta has a documented history of data extraction, surveillance, and user exploitation. I did not want my work — my tasks, my prompts, my outputs — feeding into that ecosystem.

## The Token Dynamic

Like ChatGPT, Manus used a token-based model. Free users had limited access. To continue working, I had to wait for tokens to reset.

| Token Model | What It Meant for Me |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Limited Free Tier | I could only work in bursts |
| Token Resets | I had to wait to continue |
| No Payment | I chose not to pay, but I stayed within limits |

I accepted these limits because I valued the tool. I worked around them.

## The Forced Transition

On August 11, 2026, I received an email from the Manus Team. The subject line read: **"ACTION REQUIRED: Some data in Evelyn will be deleted, back up data by August 23, 2026 at 7:59am SGT."**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Sender** | Manus Team <noreply@mail.manus.im> |
| **Date** | August 11, 2026 |
| **Affected Account** | `caro.evelyn@gmail.com` |
| **Data Affected** | Task data generated between December 29, 2025 and August 23, 2026 |
| **Deletion Window** | August 23, 2026 8:00 AM SGT – August 25, 2026 7:59 AM SGT |
| **Required Action** | Back up data by August 23, 2026 at 7:59 AM SGT |

The email stated:

> *"This is unavoidable as part of Manus's transition to independent operations and to comply with regulatory requirements in specific jurisdictions. You can back up your data before the deletion is executed and restore it after Manus resumes independent operations to keep using Manus."*

I followed the instructions. I backed up my data.
