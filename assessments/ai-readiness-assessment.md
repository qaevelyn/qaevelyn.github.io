# AI Readiness Assessment

**What this assessment captures**

A structured audit of an organization's AI stack against sovereignty, security, and vendor-dependency criteria. Ten categories. Four questions in each category. Forty questions total. Each question scored and documented. The output is a written report with a sovereignty score and priority remediations.

This assessment exists because most organizations cannot answer simple questions about their own AI stack:

- Where does the data physically live?
- Who can compel access to it?
- What happens if your AI vendor shuts down tomorrow?
- If the person who set up your AI leaves, can anyone else run it?

If you cannot answer these questions about your own systems, your AI posture is someone else's business decision. This assessment returns that posture to your control.

## How it works

This assessment is not a questionnaire. It is a structured interview series with the people who hold each answer — typically across IT, legal, finance, and operations — followed by a technical discovery pass where findings are verified against the systems in place.

No single person answers all forty questions. The auditor consolidates across interviews into a single sovereignty score.

---

## What you receive

A written report containing:

- Every question answered with a score and a finding
- A consolidated sovereignty score (0–100)
- Ten category scores
- Priority remediations, ranked by impact
- Quick wins, actionable in under 30 days
- Structural recommendations
