Intro / Story

I started as an AI tourist. I was curious, I asked questions, I explored. Then I learned, and I built. Every time I learned something new, I built it. A pipeline. Then a cleaner one. Then a different platform. Then agency. Then verification. Each ship in this fleet is a thing I learned, made real.

I built as I learned. I went from AI tourist to AI professional. This is the fleet.

How I work: I approach every system as an auditor — verify, document, don't trust. I apply the Ida B. Wells standard to everything I record: date everything, name everything, record the reasoning. I red team my own work. I test my AIs adversarially. I want to know where they break.

My direction: Use AI to audit the cloud. Niche down to security auditor.

Proof, not tenure: I hold certifications in AI, LangChain, and Agentic RAG from Google and IBM. I completed the AWS re/Start bootcamp in 3 weeks and 1 day of class time — graduated September 9, 2026.

Count my proofs.

Ship 1

Ship 1 was the first attempt — AWS, DeepSeek, a RAG pipeline built to organize my own life. It got lost. The notebook wasn't on the SageMaker instance. It wasn't in the backups. I rebuilt it from the Mirror files. The rebuild was cleaner than the original. That's the ship that taught me how to recover from losing one.

Ship 2

By the time Ship 2 came, I was more confident. I had rebuilt Ship 1 from scratch. I understood what I was capable of and what the tools could do. Ship 2 was IBM Granite — a different model, a cleaner notebook, and a new layer: agentic reasoning. I learned to write clean code and I learned what agency meant. This is the ship where I stopped being a student and started being a builder.

Ship 5

Ship 5 is the leap. IBM Granite via Ollama, running fully local. ChromaDB for retrieval. EvidenceFlow verification — every claim traceable to an evidence ID, fail-closed if evidence is missing. Built for genealogy, where a plausible guess is not good enough. This is the ship I built for the work that matters.
