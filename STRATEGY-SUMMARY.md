# Engagement summary — A Mirror of My Becoming™

Public summary of how the practice is structured. The detailed targeting and
lead-gen documents are held privately by design (owner ruling 2026-10-26,
"do both": raw detail private, public summary canonical; enforced by the push guard).

## Who the work serves

People and teams who ship AI-assisted work and need it verified before it
reaches a client, a patient, or the public record — with emphasis on
documentation in regulated and care-facing settings.

## Engagement shapes

- Audits of AI-assisted work — what a system claims vs. what it does
- Adversarial testing cycles — reproducible, documented step by step
- Documentation QA — line-by-line verification before publication
- Platform evaluation — criteria-first, workload-specific
